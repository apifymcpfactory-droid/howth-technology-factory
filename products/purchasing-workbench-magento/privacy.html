Purchasing & Receiving Workbench

Privacy notice · Effective 18 November 2026

Records in the merchant’s store

Purchasing & Receiving Workbench runs inside the merchant’s Magento installation. It records purchase orders, supplier names and references, optional supplier contact emails and notes, SKUs, source codes, prices, terms, quantities, receipts and discrepancies. Order history includes staff Admin IDs, timestamps and the supplier terms associated with the order. Delivery notes can identify the person assigned to follow up.

The merchant determines why these records are created, who may access them and how long they are kept. Magento Admin permissions govern the workbench. The extension uses the signed-in Admin session and can retain form entries and a pending receipt request there while the staff member corrects a form or checks an uncertain result. A failed operation can log its operation name and exception class in the merchant’s Magento logs.

The supplied extension sends no purchasing records or usage telemetry to HTF. It uses the merchant’s Magento database and inventory services. Its normal operation requires no HTF-hosted service. Staff choose whether to download a CSV, print an order or send it to a supplier. Those copies, store backups and any services the merchant configures are under the merchant’s control.

The extension has no automatic retention period for purchasing history. Deactivating a supplier or cancelling an order preserves historical records. Requests about information held in a merchant’s store should go to that merchant. HTF cannot retrieve or erase those records through the extension.

Marketplace checkout and support

Adobe operates the Marketplace account, order and checkout under Adobe’s privacy policy. The extension does not collect payment card details. If you contact HTF, Fred Jones trading as Howth Technology Factory handles the contact details and information you provide to answer your enquiry and administer the case. For purchasers seeking contractual support, processing is necessary to provide it. For other correspondents, HTF’s legitimate interest is responding to the enquiry they initiated.

Email providers process the correspondence. The optional HTF website form forwards enquiries by email through FormSubmit. The HTF website privacy notice describes that form and the website’s separate analytics. Send a short, redacted example if needed. Keep credentials, customer lists and database backups out of support messages.

HTF retains correspondence as needed to resolve the enquiry and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.
