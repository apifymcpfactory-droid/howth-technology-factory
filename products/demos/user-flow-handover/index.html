What this map can and cannot show

Prototype navigation between inspected screen frames is supported, including conditional possibilities. Variable values are not evaluated. Component-state changes and scroll actions are excluded. Back, close and external actions are listed in the review, not drawn as screen arrows. Routes are editable vectors. Moving cards does not automatically reconnect paths.
