What are the limits?

Up to 40 source assets and 160 planned output files. Each output is limited to 8,192 pixels per side and 16 megapixels; batches are limited to 100 megapixels and 80 MB including the delivery ZIP. Extreme crops may exceed the safe source-render budget. Use fewer assets, smaller dimensions or contain fitting.

Does this replace every advanced image tool?

This product focuses on checked delivery packs. It does not include animation or video export, advanced photo retouching, CMYK print production, AVIF or a cloud asset service. Review final image quality and target sizes before sending files to a client.

Privacy and data

The plugin allows no external network domains. HTF does not upload or store your designs. Figma manages its own platform services. Recipes are saved using Figma client storage on your device. Exported files and reports can contain confidential artwork, design names and identifiers; share them appropriately.

The browser demo uses fictional sample artwork and simulated access. It never connects to your Figma file or processes payments. This marketing page uses website analytics under our privacy notice.
