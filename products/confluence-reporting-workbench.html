The fictional browser demo does not connect to Confluence, process data through an external service or save reports in persistent browser storage. The installed app uses page-based access and Atlassian-hosted Forge storage. Read the product privacy notice, documentation and customer terms and DPA for permissions, supported limits and retention/deletion details. This page and its static assets follow the website privacy notice.
