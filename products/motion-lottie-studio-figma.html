Know what it supports.

Uses its own explicit motion settings. It does not extract Figma native Motion timelines or include a stock animation library. Text, arbitrary paths, images, effects, masks, strokes and complex compositing are unsupported and reported. Up to 200 supported shapes and 1,200 frames. Native and independent Lottie-player compatibility testing is still in progress.
