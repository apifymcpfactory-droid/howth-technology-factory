Give the next person more than a recording.

Your editor needs to know which moments matter. The next operator needs to know what went wrong and how you fixed it. Keep those notes with the production checklist, then share a report instead of asking someone to piece together separate messages or rely on your memory.

Check OBS before logging the session

Run Preflight checks the OBS connection and whether it reports recording or streaming. A failed check blocks the session record from starting, so a connection problem is visible before you continue.

Record the details people will ask for

Advance your checklist and add notes with timestamps as important moments happen. Record a problem and the steps you took to resolve it, so the person reading the report has the context.

Share notes in a useful format

Use the readable Markdown report for people, CSV event rows for a spreadsheet, or JSON for a technical workflow. Keep the exported files with the rest of the production.

Plan the work. Record what happens. Share the notes.

In Stream Deck settings, name the session, enter a production checklist (called a rundown in the plugin) and set the notes attached to your buttons. Start and stop recording separately in OBS.

  1. Set the checklist

    Configure the OBS connection, session title and production steps. Run Preflight, review its result and check the microphone signal and recording destination yourself.

  2. Add notes as you work

    Start the session record. Move through the checklist, timestamp important moments and log problems. After resolving a problem yourself, record what you did and confirm it.

  3. Export the session report

    Press Stop Session twice within five seconds to complete the record. Save the report files before starting a new session, ready for your editor or next operator.

Recording stays under your control. Start Session and Stop Session manage the production record. They do not start or stop OBS recording or streaming. Audio Health reads mute and volume settings; it does not listen for sound or certify that audio is present.

Questions before you choose it.

Compatibility, setup, data and what is included.

Do I need a Stream Deck device?

You need a supported way to trigger Stream Deck actions, such as supported hardware or Stream Deck Mobile. Any hardware, mobile entitlement or host software is supplied separately. The desktop editor alone is not a replacement for a usable controller.

Can I use it on a Mac?

Release 0.1 is scoped to Windows 10 or later. It does not claim macOS compatibility.

What does the OBS connection do?

It reads version, recording or streaming status, and the configured audio input's mute and volume settings. Connections are restricted to the same computer. Configure OBS WebSocket v5 and keep authentication enabled.

Does a passed preflight mean my production is ready?

No. It confirms the connection and available output status. Check live microphone signal, recording destination, available disk space and the rest of your production setup yourself.

Does it monitor OBS continuously?

No. Health and audio checks run when their actions are pressed. Do not treat the last displayed result as continuous monitoring.

What happens when something fails?

An unavailable OBS connection does not count as healthy. Incident actions let you record the problem. You carry out the repair, then enter and confirm the resolution; the plugin records that recovery.

Are session records saved?

The active session is saved locally and validated when restored. An invalid backup is rejected rather than silently treated as a valid session. An explicitly confirmed reset discards an invalid saved record. Export a completed session before preparing the next one.

Where does the handoff go?

To the absolute local folder you choose in Handoff settings. Each export gets a new folder containing JSON, Markdown and CSV files. UNC network-share and device paths are refused. Review the notes before sharing them.

Does it upload production data?

The candidate has no Howth Technology Factory account, cloud service, advertising, analytics or telemetry. OBS requests stay on the local computer. Host and operating-system services have their own policies.

What is included?

The plugin contains twelve actions, local configuration panels, a user guide, privacy and support information, the commercial licence and required third-party notices. OBS Studio and Stream Deck software or devices are separate.

Is the proposed price a subscription?

The proposed launch price is $19.49 USD one time. No purchase is currently available. The approved licence permits personal and internal business use on the buyer's linked devices and does not promise future upgrades.
