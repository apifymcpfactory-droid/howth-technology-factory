A workflow you can follow.

Preview first, review the scope, then create the result.

1. Choose your sources

Select up to 12 frames and choose a generic phone, tablet, browser or desktop shell. Keep the original design intact.

2. Compose the scene

Try three compositions, choose a background, adjust contain or cover fitting and add a caption. Save your recipe locally.

3. Create and refresh

Create separate editable scenes or a presentation sheet. Refresh the screen images from their sources while checking for conflicting manual edits.

Know what it supports.

Device shells and scene decorations are editable vectors. Screen content is a rendered image. These are original flat generic devices, not photorealistic branded hardware or a 3D mockup library. Up to 12 sources; each screenshot is capped at 2,048 px on its longest side. Review image quality before client delivery.
