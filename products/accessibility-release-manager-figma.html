A workflow you can follow.

Preview first, review the scope, then create the result.

1. Choose the release

Inspect selected frames or components, with up to four supported local colour-mode selections. Keep uncertain artwork separate from known text-contrast findings.

2. Review with context

Focus the actual layer, document accessible names, focus order and other implementation intent. Preview supported single-text-fill corrections before applying.

3. Compare and deliver

Save a labelled baseline in the document. Compare the same scope and modes on a later inspection, then export a printable HTML review, CSV or JSON evidence.

Know what it supports.

A design-review workflow, not a full WCAG audit or compliance certificate. Complex compositing, images, gradients, mixed text and ambiguous overlap require visual review. Semantic annotations describe intent; they do not implement screen-reader or keyboard behaviour. No live-site/code scanning, background monitoring, shared-variable corrections or hosted dashboard. Up to 20 roots, 2,000 layers, four inspected modes and a 1 MiB chunked baseline. Subscription checkout, renewal and cancellation remain unverified until Figma marketplace configuration.
