MCP Hiring & Company Research Tools | HTF

Bring hiring feeds and public-company research into the tools you already use. We are preparing MCP workflows that turn public records into consistent results you can search, compare and review.

Accenture Jobs MCP | HTF

Review Accenture vacancies in a consistent format, with role details and application links where the employer publishes them.

Planned workflow and pricing

Planned access: search, board retrieval, comparison against job IDs you supply, and HTML reports. Change checks identify added or removed job IDs. They do not track edited descriptions or run automatically.

Planned per-call prices: search and board retrieval $0.01; change check $0.02; HTML report $0.10. Pricing is provisional until release.

Hosted data checks and access verification are still pending. This MCPize version is unlisted and is not available to buy.
