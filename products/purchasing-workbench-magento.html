Purchasing & Receiving Workbench

Developed and in Review

Receive a delivery. See what’s still due. Record what arrived against an approved purchase order in Magento Admin, update source stock and keep the balance open for the next delivery.

From supplier offer to posted receipt

  • Compare unit cost, minimum quantity, pack size and estimated arrival before choosing a supplier.
  • Approve a purchase order, record each partial delivery and see who posted the receipt against which stock source.
  • Export outstanding purchases and posted receipts by source and date, with quantities and values in the order currency.

Store requirements and support

Tested on Magento Open Source 2.4.8-p5 and 2.4.9 with Magento Inventory Management. Use an existing inventory source and a physical SKU. Orders and receipts stay in your store’s database.
