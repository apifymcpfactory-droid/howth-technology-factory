The financial workflows below are still planned and unverified. The product has not been submitted to the marketplace and is not available to buy.

Planned workflow

Give each unpaid-invoice case a clear next step.

These are development goals. They are not available features or verified financial controls.

  • Customer collection cases: bring linked invoices and outstanding balances into a case for review.
  • Disputes and payment promises: record why an invoice is disputed and when payment has been promised.
  • Assigned next actions: show who is responsible, what needs to happen next and which work is overdue.
  • Manager review: review cases and decisions with appropriate access permissions.
  • Weekly review records: export a review summary and retain a history of case changes.

Before release

Built around the accounting record.

The intended role is to organise collection work alongside Zoho Books. Zoho Books remains the source of accounting records; this product is not intended to replace its ledger.

Invoice access, saved cases, role permissions, organisation separation and the complete review workflow still require implementation and testing. Supported customer editions and regional availability have not been confirmed.

Pricing and release timing have not been announced. Contact HTC about the planned product.
