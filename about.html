About

Practical tools, narrowly scoped.

Howth Technology Factory is based in Dublin, Ireland. We build focused software utilities that do one job clearly for people, developers, teams and AI agents.

What we build

Our work includes developer tools, automation utilities, MCP-compatible tools, AI-agent tools, validation tools, compliance utilities and document tooling.

Where to use them

Products are available through Apify and MCPize, so they can be used directly or connected to compatible automated and agent workflows.

How we work

We focus on narrowly scoped, practical tools instead of broad platforms. Each product is designed around a defined task and a clear result.
